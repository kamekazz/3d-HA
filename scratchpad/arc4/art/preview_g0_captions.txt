Extrapolating to all 64 tiles of the room, a SINGLE shared atlas at tile 256 is
~1.4 MB and a2kit embeds it in each of Cabinets East / North / South, i.e. three
times.  That breaks the room's 1.5 MB budget on its own.  Two levers, both the
integrator's call:
  * TILE 128 costs ~8 KB/tile and still resolves NBA JAM / CHAMPION EDITION
    (the letterforms are drawn at ~0.47 of the marquee height).
  * Split the atlas PER WALL RUN so each GLB carries only its own machines'
    tiles (east 7, south 4+1, north 4) instead of all sixteen.  At TILE 128
    that is roughly 224 / 160 / 128 KB across the three pieces.
GRAIN = False saves a further 37%; the 4x4 ordered dither alone meters
mean|d1| 7.2-18.0 at texel scale.
