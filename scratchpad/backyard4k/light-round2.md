## Actual-foliage sky-visibility experiment retained

Parent authorized the helper extension and supplied build buckets at its call site. `addRearLightDetail(buckets = null)` measures each current mature broadleaf's actual foliage bbox and projected triangle area. It corrects duplicate reverse-facing triangles, then derives porous crown coverage from projected leaf area. The 13 canopies have equivalent radii of 13.56–15.43 feet and coverage of 0.209–0.322. Deleted items contribute zero. Viewer vertices already contain saved transforms; editor centers follow the current item group's world transform, scale and visibility.

A circular-crown approximation estimates projected solid angle and combines overlapping crowns multiplicatively. Only the indirect diffuse/specular terms of the three dedicated rear turf materials change, within Z < -10.9 and Y < 8. Direct sunlight and HA lights retain their existing response. The wrapper composes existing material and render callbacks, including snow and blade-normal shaders. Ambient illumination continues to follow the app's day/night/weather lighting. No global callback, light, texture or render target was added.

The canonical 335°/42° sunny before/after captures are `renders/light-sky-before/photo09.png` and `renders/light-sky-after/photo09.png`. This is a mild improvement, well short of reference 09's shaded foreground.
