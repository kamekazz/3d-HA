What changed

  1. stoppedStopped at the owner's call during round 2 wave A. The four in-flight builders were killed, so their partial work was reverted: the wall skin deleted again (white blobs on the west wall) and the floor restored to its previous build. The ceiling and knee wall rebuilds had already landed and were kept — both are improvements. All six views re-shot and checked.
  2. repairThe killed round-2 agents left three pieces placed in debug state — the knee wall in per-part R/G/B mask colours, the baseboards in magenta and the ceiling as a calibration fan — and a fourth, the wall skins, holding a near-black panel that punched two pure-black voids into the hallway above and beside the far door. All four are repaired: the real pieces re-placed, the doors reverted to the round-1 build, and the broken wall skin deleted so the room renders on the engine's own wall grey until it is rebuilt.
  3. repairThose black voids had been present since round 1 and no critic caught them, because each critic was told to judge one subject. Round 2 adds two critics whose subject is the room as a whole.
  4. round 2Eight builders now working every verdict below, then twelve blind critics — including two judging the room as a whole rather than any one object.
  5. round 1Nine blind critics, nine failures, all certain. Four themes ran through every verdict: no texture anywhere, nothing touches the ground, every white is the same white, and no light falloff.
  6. round 1The "extra door" is answered without touching the plan. All five openings are supported by the photographs and the plan. The extra door people were seeing was a rendering artefact — every doorway carried two coincident white slabs a wall-thickness apart, ours and the neighbouring room's. That is now fixed.
  7. structuralAdded rooms.is_void and set it on room 28 "Stairwell". house.js now skips a void room's floor slab and plinth cap, so the stairwell is a hole in the second floor instead of a slab capping it. Looking down the flight now reaches the first-floor front door.
  8. structuralCut full-span floor-to-ceiling openings on room 17 edge 1 and room 28 edge 3, which makes buildRoom skip those walls outright. The full-height wall standing where the knee wall belongs is gone; the knee wall now carries the whole boundary.
  9. camerasRe-aimed all six cameras onto the re-traced footprint. The room's anchor had moved 3.8 ft west when its east arm was added, which left the old poses pointing at the wrong walls.
  10. unchangedThe floor plan is untouched — the polygon, the anchor and all five door positions are exactly as the owner signed them off. No other room has been modified.
