# Rear lawn material

Grass 01 by Dorian Zgraggen / cgbookcase, CC0 1.0.

Source: https://www.cgbookcase.com/textures/grass-01

License confirmation: https://www.dorianzgraggen.com/projects/cgbookcase/

`grass-albedo.webp` derives from the original 2K Base Color map, resized to
768 pixels and compressed as WebP. `grass-normal-dx.webp` derives from the
DirectX normal map, resized to 512 pixels and compressed as WebP. The latter
requires a negative normalScale.y in Three.js. No reference photograph is
projected into the scene. These maps are used only by the rear turf material.

## Rear tree bark

Jolcham Oak Bark 01 by Charlotte Baglioni / Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.

Source and license: https://polyhaven.com/a/jolcham_oak_bark_01

`oak-bark-albedo.webp` is the original albedo resized to 512 × 1024 pixels;
`oak-bark-normal.webp` is the OpenGL normal map resized to 384 × 768 pixels.
Both are WebP-compressed. The scanned tile covers 1 × 2 metres. These maps
are used only on the rear woodland trunks and branches.

## Rear oak leaves

Oak Leaf 05 by Dorian Zgraggen / cgbookcase, CC0 1.0.

Source: https://www.cgbookcase.com/textures/oak-leaf-05

License: https://www.dorianzgraggen.com/projects/cgbookcase/

`oak-leaf-albedo.webp` packs the original front Base Color and Opacity maps
into RGBA, resized to 384 × 655 pixels. `oak-leaf-normal-dx.webp` is the
DirectX normal map resized to 256 × 437 pixels; normalScale.y is negative.
Only format, size and channel packing changed. Each texture is mapped onto
individual curved leaves in the rear woodland, with no reference projection.
